The three rules

  1. Ask about all four. Every client gets asked about the other group companies.
  2. Log it the same day. Every lead and call goes on this log before you go home.
  3. Act within one day. A referred lead is contacted within one working day, and the referrer hears back.

Response times

  • Contact in 1 working day, visit in 3, quote within 2 of the visit.
  • Update status weekly so the referrer never has to chase.
  • Brand-new prospect: first to log it owns it. Lapses after 90 days with no activity.
  • Can't act within a working day? Say so in Group Leads so someone else can.

Never

  • Sell to insurer clients (see list below).
  • Promise prices or dates for a sister company.
  • Go around a plumbing partner. Leeukoppie and Orchards go through Dean (Atlantic Plumbing).
  • Sit on a lead.

Who owns the client

  • Existing DC24 client: Jayd
  • Existing Leakfind commercial or relining client: Bianca / Marco / Shane
  • Existing Climacore client: Lance
  • New DC24 client Bianca closes: Jayd stays account lead, Bianca earns the 4%
  • Brand new prospect: first to log it (lapses after 90 days)
  • Client of more than one company: the company with the highest sales
  • Insurer-referred policyholder: not cross-sold

DC24 quote in 5 steps

  1. Lead in, site assessed
  2. Bianca does the Data Box report and QuickBooks quote
  3. Jayd checks it
  4. Quotes Group approves
  5. Quote goes to the client
